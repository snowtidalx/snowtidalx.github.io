LunarKeep

LunarKeep keeps dates you choose on the Chinese lunisolar calendar and can remind you on those days.

  • Events, lists, notes, language and settings stay on your device.
  • No account. No sign-in.
  • Local notifications fire on the device. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • If you tap Sync, the app may write all-day dates into a calendar you pick on the phone. We do not receive that calendar.
  • If you tap Backup, you get an encrypted file. You choose where it goes (Files, iCloud, Drive). We do not host it.
  • In-app purchase, if offered, is handled by Apple or Google. We do not receive your card number.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

LunarKeep may show banner ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). Banners hide if you unlock the app (Remove ads).

We do not send your events, lists, notes, or backup file to Google or to snowtidalx. The ad SDK talks to Google on its own.

Google may collect, to show and measure ads:

  • Approximate location (from IP address)
  • Device ID (advertising identifier if you allow tracking; otherwise an app-scoped ID)
  • Advertising data (ads shown or tapped)
  • Product interaction (ad impressions)
  • Crash logs and performance data

Personalized ads may use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can refuse. Ads can still show without tracking.

Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How AdMob uses data: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run our own analytics or crash service in LunarKeep.
  • We do not require a server account to use the app.
